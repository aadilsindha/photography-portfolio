Questions

How far in advance should I book?

Two to four weeks is ideal. Weekends fill up first, especially in wedding season.

When do I get my photos?

Portraits within 7 days, events within 2 weeks. A small preview comes sooner.

Do you travel outside Johannesburg?

Yes. Travel costs are added to the quote for shoots outside Gauteng.

Can I print and share the images?

Yes. Personal sharing and printing is included. Commercial use is agreed up front.
